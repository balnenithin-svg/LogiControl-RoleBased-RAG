
DOCUMENT: Shipment Tracking SOP
DOCUMENT_ID: LOG-TRACK-001
CATEGORY: Shipment Tracking

PURPOSE:
This document defines the standard process for tracking shipments and monitoring delivery progress.

1. SHIPMENT IDENTIFICATION
- Identify shipments using their unique Order ID.
- Verify shipment details before providing tracking information.
- Check the shipment status in the logistics system.

2. TRACKING PROCESS
- Review the latest available shipment tracking information.
- Check recorded locations and timestamps.
- Review shipment milestones and estimated delivery dates.
- Identify missing or outdated tracking information.

3. SHIPMENT STATUS
- Pending: Shipment is awaiting processing.
- Processing: Shipment is being prepared.
- Shipped: Shipment has been dispatched.
- In Transit: Shipment is moving through the delivery network.
- Delivered: Shipment has reached its destination.
- Delayed: Shipment has not progressed according to its schedule.

4. TRACKING EXCEPTIONS
- Investigate missing tracking updates.
- Verify the last recorded location and timestamp.
- Escalate unresolved tracking issues to the Operations team.

5. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION
- Provide customers with verified tracking information.
- Do not describe historical GPS records as live locations.
- Communicate uncertainty when tracking information is unavailable.

END OF DOCUMENT
