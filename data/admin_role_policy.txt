LOG-ADMIN-001: ADMIN ROLE POLICY

1. User Management
- Create and manage internal user accounts.
- Assign roles: Admin, Operations, Dispatcher, Customer Support.
- Control access permissions.
- Disable unauthorized or inactive accounts.

2. Access Control
- Ensure users can access only their permitted information.
- Maintain role-based access security.
- Protect confidential shipment and customer information.

3. System Monitoring
- Monitor overall platform activity.
- Review operational reports and system performance.
- Identify access or system-related issues.

4. Escalation Management
- Monitor critical unresolved operational issues.
- Coordinate with the Operations Manager.
- Ensure escalated issues are tracked until resolution.

5. Security
- Protect login credentials and authentication tokens.
- Review access permissions regularly.
- Report suspicious access activity.
