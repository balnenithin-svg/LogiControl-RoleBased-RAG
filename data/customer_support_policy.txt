
DOCUMENT: Customer Support and Communication Policy
DOCUMENT_ID: LOG-CUSTOMER-004
CATEGORY: Customer Support

PURPOSE:
This policy defines how Customer Support handles shipment enquiries, delivery updates, complaints, and customer communication.

1. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION
- Verify the customer's identity using the approved verification process.
- Confirm the relevant Order ID.
- Never disclose another customer's shipment information.

2. SHIPMENT ENQUIRIES
- Check the latest available shipment status.
- Review verified tracking records.
- Provide accurate delivery information.
- Explain when tracking information is unavailable.

3. DELAY COMMUNICATION
- Inform customers about confirmed delivery delays.
- Explain verified reasons for delays.
- Never invent delay reasons or delivery dates.
- Provide revised delivery estimates only when confirmed.

4. CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS
- Record the customer's complaint.
- Identify the related shipment and issue.
- Forward operational issues to the Operations team.
- Track the complaint until resolution.

5. ESCALATION
- Routine enquiries: Customer Support handles directly.
- Tracking problems: Escalate to Operations.
- Dispatch problems: Coordinate with the Dispatcher.
- Critical unresolved complaints: Escalate to the responsible manager.

6. PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION
- Use clear and respectful language.
- Avoid unsupported promises.
- Protect customer information.
- Maintain records of customer interactions.

7. ISSUE CLOSURE
- Confirm the resolution with available records.
- Update the customer interaction history.
- Close the enquiry after resolution.

END OF DOCUMENT
