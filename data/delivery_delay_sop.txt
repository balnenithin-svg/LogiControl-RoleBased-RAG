
DOCUMENT: Delivery Delay Management SOP
DOCUMENT_ID: LOG-DELAY-002
CATEGORY: Delay Management

PURPOSE:
This document defines the standard procedure for identifying, investigating, and resolving delayed shipments.

1. DELAY IDENTIFICATION
- Compare actual shipment progress with the scheduled delivery timeline.
- Identify shipments that have exceeded their expected delivery milestones.
- Record the Order ID and delay duration.

2. DELAY INVESTIGATION
- Check the latest available shipment status.
- Review historical tracking locations and timestamps.
- Verify dispatch and delivery records.
- Identify possible causes such as traffic, weather, warehouse delays, or operational issues.
- Do not assume a cause without supporting evidence.

3. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
- Contact the responsible operations team.
- Coordinate with the dispatcher.
- Evaluate alternative delivery arrangements when appropriate.
- Record the investigation findings and actions taken.

4. ESCALATION
- Escalate unresolved delays to the Operations Manager.
- Escalate critical customer-impacting delays to the appropriate support team.
- Maintain an investigation record for every escalated shipment.

5. CUSTOMER UPDATES
- Inform Customer Support about verified delay information.
- Communicate revised delivery estimates only when confirmed.
- Maintain clear and professional communication.

6. RESOLUTION
- Verify that the shipment has resumed progress or been delivered.
- Update the shipment investigation record.
- Close the exception after confirmation.

END OF DOCUMENT
