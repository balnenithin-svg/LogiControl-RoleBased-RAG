
DOCUMENT: Dispatch and Escalation Policy
DOCUMENT_ID: LOG-DISPATCH-003
CATEGORY: Dispatch Management

PURPOSE:
This policy defines dispatch responsibilities, shipment coordination, and escalation procedures.

1. DISPATCH RESPONSIBILITIES
- Review shipment orders before dispatch.
- Verify destination, delivery details, and assigned resources.
- Confirm shipment readiness before dispatch.
- Update dispatch status in the logistics system.

2. DISPATCH PROCESS
- Receive the confirmed shipment order.
- Verify package and delivery information.
- Assign the available delivery resource.
- Record dispatch time and shipment details.
- Notify Operations about dispatch exceptions.

3. ROUTE COORDINATION
- Review available route information.
- Consider distance, traffic, and delivery priorities.
- Coordinate route changes with the Operations team.
- Record approved route changes.

4. EXCEPTION HANDLING
- Investigate failed dispatch attempts.
- Report unavailable delivery resources.
- Escalate incorrect shipment information.
- Record the reason for every dispatch exception.

5. ESCALATION LEVELS
- Level 1: Dispatcher investigates routine operational issues.
- Level 2: Operations team handles unresolved dispatch problems.
- Level 3: Operations Manager handles critical or recurring exceptions.

6. RECORD KEEPING
- Maintain dispatch records.
- Record assigned resources and timestamps.
- Document escalation decisions.
- Update the shipment record after resolution.

END OF DOCUMENT
